**Main Content:**

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from cyberattacks, unauthorized access, and digital threats. In today's technology-driven world, cybersecurity is essential for individuals, businesses, and organizations.

Common cyber threats include phishing, malware, ransomware, password attacks, and data breaches. These attacks can cause financial losses, expose personal information, and disrupt business operations.

Cybersecurity includes network security, application security, cloud security, and information security. Technologies such as firewalls, encryption, antivirus software, and multifactor authentication help protect digital systems.

To stay safe online, users should create strong passwords, enable multifactor authentication, update software regularly, avoid suspicious links, and back up important data.

Artificial intelligence is also improving cybersecurity by detecting unusual activities and identifying potential threats faster. However, cybercriminals can misuse AI to develop more sophisticated attacks.

In conclusion, cybersecurity is essential for maintaining privacy, protecting sensitive information, and building a safer digital future.
